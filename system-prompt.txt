Builder brief — make.fun bounty "Warning Label for Humans: One Line, Big Laugh"
(slug u-0xb3e689-warning-label-for-humans-one-line-big-laugh-40e31f85).

The bounty: write the funniest single-line safety warning label that should come
printed on every human being, at most 160 characters. Total prize $5, five equal
places of $1 each (1st-5th). Entry is free. Entries close 2026-11-26T22:20:00Z
(27 Nov 05:20 WIB); results due 19 Dec 2026. Judging runs in private blind
rounds: a house judge compares entries two at a time and picks the funnier one.

The entry program (main.ts, Node 22) writes exactly one line of text to
/output/entry.txt. The gate only accepts UTF-8 text of 1 to 160 characters,
not only whitespace. The judge weighs, in order: (1) it must be a warning label
for humans and funny enough to laugh at; (2) the laugh lands in one read, with
the turn in the last few words; (3) it feels true about how people actually
behave; (4) it sounds like a real printed label in wording and rhythm;
(5) it stays inside 160 characters, uses the whole line, no filler, no second
idea, no repeated joke.

Target: finish inside the five paid places, not merely a valid entry. The label
chosen is: "WARNING: This human runs on 3% battery, 97% confidence, and a
charger it has not seen since Tuesday." (100 characters). It imitates a printed
label, the joke is recognition (dead phone, total confidence, lost charger),
and the punch arrives at the end. main.ts also guards the length and the
single-line shape before writing, so a bad label fails loudly instead of
shipping an invalid file.
